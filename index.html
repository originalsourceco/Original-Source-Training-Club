What we're about

You've tried to make training a permanent habit more than once. The 5am alarms. The 12-week programs. The disengaged PT you signed up with who pushed too hard until you stopped showing up. The new year reset that lasted until February. Every time it felt forced, and became impossible to maintain. It was a repeating pattern, and somewhere along the way you started to think the problem was you. It isn't.

The problem was an approach to training that fought your life instead of fitting around it. Here, I'll teach you to train with less friction: sessions that suit your schedule, effort that suits your energy levels, and a plan you can keep for years, not weeks.

You'll get to a place where you'll have a toolkit to deal with all the road bumps that arise when trying to train consistently, such as a bad night's sleep, low motivation, or a stressful work life. A missed session gets adjusted, not made up. A short day still counts. You train in a way you actually enjoy, and the results come the easiest way possible. Along the way you build confidence in yourself, until you don't need a coach to keep you going.

Questions

Common questions.

What's the difference between OS Training Club and individual coaching?

OS Training Club gives you a new program every month with three session options a day, and a group chat with Nick. Individual coaching is a custom program built around your goal, written in four-week blocks, with check-ins usually twice a week.

What goals can you help with?

OS Training Club is a group training program with a community of like-minded people. It's for you if you want to build and keep good all-round fitness, strength and mobility, with support there when you need it. It will also lift your ability in running, rowing, HYROX, CrossFit, sport, hypertrophy and weight loss.

If you're working towards a specific goal or event, choose individual coaching. Your program is written around your goal and your circumstances, with closer, more specific support along the way.

Is the coaching online?

Yes. Everything is remote, so you train wherever you are, whenever suits you. There are no class times.

Will I get enough support to achieve my goals?

Yes. This way of coaching is tried and tested, and you'll feel supported from the start. You get a program that suits your circumstances, and a coach who genuinely understands what you're going through and the roadblocks you're likely to face.
