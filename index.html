OS Training Club

The monthly group program

One program a month, three sessions a day. Do one, two or all three.

  • A new program every month, written by Nick.
  • Three session options a day across strength, cardio, plyometrics and explosive work.
  • You choose the sessions that fit your goals, and your cardio: run, row, ride, swim or ski.
  • Train by feel. Effort set by how it feels, not numbers you can't read yet.
  • A community that backs you. Like-minded people training their own way and supporting each other. No ego, no pressure to perform.
  • Group chat with Nick. Ask a question, get a real answer.
  • Any time, anywhere. No class times. Join any month.

For you if you want structure and a coach in your corner, train for more than one thing, or have fallen off programs before.

Individual coaching

Programming built around you

A program for your goal, your week and your body, with Nick in your corner.

  • A quick call first on your goals and where you're at.
  • A custom program for any goal: strength, running, rowing, HYROX, CrossFit, sport, hypertrophy or weight loss.
  • Four-week blocks, reviewed and adjusted as you go.
  • Check-ins, usually Monday and Thursday, plus voice notes.
  • Remote, wherever you train.

For you if you have a specific goal or event, an injury history to work around, or want close accountability.
