02 · This month

Every month has a focus.

Each program opens with training notes that explain what the month is for and how the four weeks build. You always know why you're doing what you're doing.

  • Week 1 settles in, week 3 is the big one
  • Week 4 eases off with a benchmark to beat next month

03 · The week

Three options, every day.

Each week is laid out Monday to Sunday. Every day gives you a strength option, an engine option and a power and conditioning option. Do one, two or all three.

  • A: Strength. Weights, 35 to 50 minutes
  • B: Engine. Run, row, ride, swim or ski
  • C: Power and conditioning. Jumps, sprints, circuits

04 · A session

Open any session and go.

Tap a session and it's all there: the exercises, sets, reps and effort for each week, with a column for every week of the month. Effort is set by feel, so it scales to your day.

02 · Your week

It starts with your week.

Before a single session gets written, you fill in what a normal week of work and life looks like. The program is built to fit around it, so training doesn't add stress to your schedule.

03 · Your program

Four-week blocks, explained.

Your program is written in four-week blocks. Each block comes with notes on what changed and why, and how much easy and hard work is in it, so you can see the progression.

  • Notes from Nick on every block
  • Total, easy and hard minutes tracked
  • Adjusted as you go, when life changes

04 · A block
